Here’s what each plot is showing and how the legends map to the data:

training_points_latentspace.png

- Overall: Latent-space scatter of the first 1,000 training images, reduced to 2D
  (PCA). Focus is on characterizing the training distribution and highlighting
  outliers/inliers.
- Legend entries:
    - mapped train pts (blue, semi‑transparent): all mapped training points.
    - regen images (cyan): the first 5 of those training points, singled out so you can
      match them to regenerated images later.
    - top outliers (orange, numbered): the most distant points from the mean in the PCA
      space.
    - close to mean (chartreuse, numbered): the points closest to the mean in the PCA
      space.

compare_points_2d.png

- Overall: Latent-space scatter comparing training points, simulated samples, and
  “other” (validation) images, again in 2D via PCA. Focus is on how simulations and
  holdout data align with the training distribution.
- Legend entries:
    - train images (blue, semi‑transparent): the mapped training points.
    - regen images (cyan): the first 5 training points, flagged similarly.
    - sim images (orange, numbered): latent points used to generate simulated images
      (numbers correspond to annotated points).
    - anomaly images (chartreuse, numbered): mapped “other”/validation images.

Key difference: the first plot highlights distribution diagnostics within the training
set (outliers vs inliers), while the second compares training to simulated and holdout/
validation points; no outlier/inlier highlight there.


How the PNGs line up with the plots:

- outlier_image_*.png: regenerated from the top_outliers latent points. Those points
  are the orange, numbered “top outliers” in training_points_latentspace.png. Their
  numbers match the annotations in that plot.
- inlier_image_*.png: regenerated from closest_to_mean latent points. Those are the
  chartreuse, numbered “close to mean” points in training_points_latentspace.png, with
  matching numbers.
- regen_image_*.png: regenerated from mapped_training_pts[14:]. These aren’t explicitly
  highlighted in either plot (the “regen images” legend entries in the plots correspond
  to the first 5 training points, not to these files), so there’s no one-to-one visual
  match.
- sim_image_*.png: generated from the sim_pts used in compare_points_2d.png; the
  orange, numbered “sim images” points there match these files.

There are no saved PNGs for the “anomaly images” (validation/other points); they’re
only shown as chartreuse points in compare_points_2d.png.
